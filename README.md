# VO₂ Breathe PWA v7.12

Breathing-library review build before the Swift migration.

## 11-protocol library

RECOVER
- Recovery 5.5 — 5.5 in / 5.5 out · 6 min
- Recovery Downshift — 4 in / 6 out · 5 min
- Sleep Downshift — 4 in / 6 out · 10 min
- Resonance 6 — 4 in / 6 out · 10 min

PERFORM
- Performance Primer — 4/4 → 3/3 → 2/2 across 3 min
- Morning Rise — 2/2 → 3/3 → normal breathing across 5 min

FOCUS
- Box 4 — 4 in / 4 hold / 4 out / 4 hold · 5 min
- Extended Box — 4 in / 4 hold / 8 out / 4 hold · 6 min
- 4–7–8 Downshift — 4 in / 7 hold / 8 out · 6 cycles

TRAIN
- BOLT Reset — comfortable post-exhale holds with recovery
- Hypoxic Tolerance — fixed 6 / 8 / 10 / 12 sec exhale holds · Advanced

## Training-plan integration
- Interval days: Performance Primer before + Recovery Downshift after.
- Short Zone 2: Recovery Downshift after.
- Longer Zone 2: Recovery 5.5 after.
- Dedicated breath days: Resonance 6.
- Consolidation/reset breath days: Box 4.
- Recovery days: Recovery 5.5.
- Hypoxic Tolerance and BOLT Reset are intentionally not auto-prescribed in the general aerobic plan.

## Interaction details
- Box and Extended Box now keep the wave large during the post-inhale hold and small during the post-exhale hold.
- Performance Primer and Morning Rise change cadence as the session progresses.
- Hypoxic Tolerance uses a deterministic 60-second preparation followed by fixed 6, 8, 10, and 12 second holds with 60-second recovery periods.
- Hypoxic Tolerance shows a one-time safety acknowledgement.
- BOLT Reset remains comfort-limited; the displayed hold is a conservative ceiling rather than a target.
- v7.11 main-tab swipe navigation, fixed carousel dots, Today reordering, settings behavior, and Forest Nebula assets are preserved.
